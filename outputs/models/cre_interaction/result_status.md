# CRE concentration interaction

The primary regression includes bank and quarter fixed effects, lagged controls, the time-varying lagged CRE/Tier1 main effect, and its interaction with CRE year-over-year price growth. Quarter FE absorb the national shock main effect; identification is cross-sectional and is not presented as strictly causal. The primary growth interaction estimate is -2.8580145e-06 with bank-clustered SE 1.3374784e-05 and 95% CI [-2.9072592e-05, 2.3356563e-05]. For a decline-magnitude convention the sign is reversed, not reinterpreted as new evidence. The CRE-share and forward-used pre-2022 exposure variants were pre-authorized; no significance search was performed.
