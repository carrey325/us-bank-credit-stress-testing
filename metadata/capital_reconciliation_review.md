# R1 capital-ratio reconciliation review

All 2,695 evaluable core-bank quarters pass the existing one-basis-point check
of mapped Tier 1 capital divided by mapped RWA against independently reported
item 7206. The tolerance was not widened and reported source values were not
altered.

Two non-core candidate-bank observations outside the downstream panel showed
small early-period source differences during the broader mapping scan. They do
not affect the core panel and are not presented as passing core observations.
